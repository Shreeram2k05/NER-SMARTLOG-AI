About NER-SMARTLOG AI

NER-SMARTLOG AI is an enterprise-grade AI-Based Smart Logistics & Accessibility Intelligence Platform engineered specifically for the distinct geographical vulnerabilities of the North Eastern Region of India.

It integrates terrain vulnerability indexes, real-time weather and landslide prediction models, synchromodal transport (Road, Rail, Air), and autonomous emergency rerouting to guarantee lifeline supply distribution during monsoon blockades and disasters.

Designed for:
  • Smart India Hackathon & Government Technology Demonstrations
  • State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA) of the 8 NER States
  • North Eastern Regional Agricultural Marketing Corporation (NERAMAC)
  • Border Roads Organisation (BRO) and National Highways Authority (NHAI)
